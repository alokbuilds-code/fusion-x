SECTION 03 // MAGNETIC TOPOLOGY

Magnetic Confinement & Helical Drift

A pure toroidal magnetic field suffers from Grad-B and curvature drifts, which separate positive ions and negative electrons vertically, creating an electric field that blows the plasma into the outer wall. Tokamaks induce a poloidal field to twist lines into closed helices, neutralizing charge separation.

Magnetic Mirror Effect & Neoclassical Transport

Due to toroidal geometry, the magnetic field is stronger on the inboard side ($B \propto 1/R$). Particles with high perpendicular velocity bounce between high-field regions, tracing banana-shaped orbits that increase neoclassical diffusion.

NUCLEAR REACTION MECHANICS

Fusing the Lightest Nuclei to Power Humanity

Nuclear fusion is the fundamental energy source of the universe. When two light hydrogen isotopes—Deuterium (²H) and Tritium (³H)—collide with sufficient energy to overcome electrostatic Coulomb repulsion, they fuse into Helium-4 and release a high-energy neutron.

⚛
Mass Defect Energy Release: $\Delta m = m_D + m_T - (m_\alpha + m_n) = 0.0188 \text{ u}$. Converted via $E = \Delta m c^2$ to produce exactly 17.6 MeV per reaction.
⚡
Energy Density: One gram of deuterium-tritium fuel yields the energy equivalent of 10,000 liters of oil, with zero greenhouse gas emissions and no long-lived radioactive waste.
🌊
Inexhaustible Fuel: Deuterium is naturally abundant in seawater (33 g/m³). Tritium is bred directly inside the reactor using lithium blankets ($^6\text{Li} + n \to ^4\text{He} + \text{T} + 4.8 \text{ MeV}$).
PULSE CYCLE DYNAMICS

The 6 Steps of a Tokamak Discharge

Because magnetic coils cannot run continuous inductive current without saturation, standard tokamaks operate in discrete pulses or utilize radiofrequency current drive for steady-state burn.

01
Vacuum Pumping & Gas Puff: Chamber pumped to 10⁻⁹ mbar; a minute puff of D-T fuel gas (milligrams) is admitted.
02
Inductive Breakdown: Central solenoid pulses a steep voltage spike, ionizing the gas into a conductive plasma.
03
Ohmic Heating & Ramp-up: 15 MA current heats plasma up to 20–30 Million K via electrical resistance.
04
Auxiliary Heating & H-Mode: 50 MW of neutral beams and microwaves trigger the High-Confinement edge barrier, reaching 150 MK.
05
Burning Flat-top: Self-heating alpha particles maintain temperature while fusion power outputs 500 MW.
MAGNETIC EQUILIBRIUM

Overcoming Charge Drifts with Helical Shear

In curved magnetic fields, the gradient in magnetic field strength ($B \propto 1/R$) and field curvature induce cross-field drifts:

v_drift = (m / q B²) * [ v_parallel² (R × B)/R² + 0.5 v_perp² (B × ∇B)/B ]

Positive ions drift upward while negative electrons drift downward. The resulting vertical polarization generates an electric field $\mathbf{E}$ that causes rapid $\mathbf{E} \times \mathbf{B}$ outward expulsion. By adding the poloidal field, field lines connect the top and bottom, allowing short-circuit current along field lines to neutralize the charge separation!

TRIPLE PRODUCT FORMULATION

The Triple Product Ignition Threshold

Formulated by British physicist John D. Lawson in 1955, the triple product dictates that for fusion self-heating to exceed energy losses, the product of density, temperature, and energy confinement time must satisfy:

n · T · τ_E ≥ 3.0 × 10²¹ keV · s · m⁻³

MATERIALS & TECHNOLOGY FRONTIERS

The Modern Engineering Bottlenecks

Overcoming the physics of fusion has shifted from pure plasma science to hardcore mechanical, materials, and cryogenic engineering.

01
High-Temperature Superconductors (HTS): Rare-Earth Barium Copper Oxide (REBCO) tape operates at 20+ Tesla at 20 Kelvin, enabling reactors to be dramatically more compact.
02
14 MeV Neutron Material Damage: High-energy neutrons displace atoms in stainless steel, causing lattice swelling and embrittlement. Developing Reduced-Activation Ferritic Martensitic (RAFM) steels is vital.
03
Divertor Heat Flux (20 MW/m²): Exceeding the thermal re-entry load of a spacecraft. Tungsten monoblocks with sub-surface water cooling channels operating near boiling margins.
04
Tritium Breeding Blankets: Closed-loop fuel cycle requiring a Tritium Breeding Ratio (TBR) $> 1.05$ using lithium ceramics or liquid lead-lithium eutectic alloys.
